Frequently asked questions

Is Infinitodo really free?

Yes. The core to do list app, habit tracker, and reminder app features are completely free to use — no trial timer, no surprise paywall on the basics.

Can I use Infinitodo on mobile?

Absolutely. Infinitodo is fully responsive — bottom navigation on phones, a sidebar on desktop, and the same data on every device.

How does the habit tracker work?

Add the habits you want to build, check them off each day, and Infinitodo automatically calculates your current streak, your personal best, and how close you are to beating it.

Will I actually get notified about my reminders?

Yes. The reminder app shows a full-screen alert with sound when a reminder fires, so a quiet notification never costs you the meeting again.

Does this work as a daily planner?

That is exactly what it is built for. The dashboard combines your tasks for today, your habit check-ins, your reminders, and a productivity score — your full day on one screen.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored securely and only you can see it. Infinitodo never sells data and only uses it to power your productivity tool experience.
