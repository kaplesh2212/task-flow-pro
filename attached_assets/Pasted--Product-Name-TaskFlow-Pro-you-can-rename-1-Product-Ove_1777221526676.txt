🧠 Product Name: TaskFlow Pro (you can rename)
1. 🎯 Product Overview

TaskFlow Pro is a productivity app that combines:

✅ To-Do List Manager
🔁 Habit Tracker
⏰ Smart Reminder System

The goal is to help users stay consistent, organized, and productive with minimal friction and a clean UI.

2. 🎯 Target Audience
Students (like you 👀)
Freelancers
Working professionals
Self-improvement enthusiasts
